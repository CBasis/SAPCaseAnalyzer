Switch to other case

  1. Open the SAP case in SAP4Me and choose Export Case in the upper-right corner.
  2. On the exported page, press Ctrl+A to select the full page content, then Ctrl+C to copy it.
  3. Return here and paste into the text field with Ctrl+V, or choose Take from clipboard.
  4. Choose Analyze case to switch the dashboard to that case.
